Connect your touchpad and keyboard

In the presentation browser, choose “Use phone as touchpad and keyboard” and open its controller link here. Keep the private connection available on both devices.

A desktop connection code cannot authorize a controller. Controller links are one use and expire after five minutes.

Slide one finger to move. Tap to click. Two fingers scroll. Arrow keys move the pointer; Enter clicks.

Options and connection

Desktop video and audio play only in the presentation browser. This controller plays no audio. App-only casting must be checked on your phone and display.
